How this works & what to try

The brain. Before every game, the person's network looks at 9 features: how much they trust this partner (memory + gossip), the partner's last move, their own last move, the partner's recent cooperation rate, how well they know each other, their own recent average payoff, whether they sent the invite, how late in history it is, and a grudge flag — 1 if this partner ever betrayed them. Output: probability of cooperating. Learning is Monte-Carlo REINFORCE: after each meeting (a run of back-to-back games with one partner), every move is credited with the discounted payoff stream it helped cause. A second smaller brain learns when to accept invites — slowly, because trust is stubborn. Gossip is a club good: people only share intel with those they trust. And one social norm is hard-wired and fully transparent: the retaliation reflex — nobody can bring themselves to cooperate with someone who just betrayed them (pride). The brain still learns everything else; the reflex just makes punishment reliable, so defection is credibly deterred from round one.

The headline finding. With the retaliation norm in place, blank-slate selfish minds reliably build a thriving civilization: ~100% cooperation, ~98% of invites accepted, on every seed — under harsh, mild, and textbook payoffs. Turn the reflex off (No honor preset) and the same minds collapse into mutual suspicion (~15% cooperation, ~2% acceptance): intelligence alone finds the Nash trap. The difference isn't the payoffs or the brains — it's the institution. Reliable retaliation turns "betray" from a tempting gamble into a guaranteed loss, and self-interest does the rest.

Things to try:

  • Reality (defaults): run several seeds. Watch cooperation climb to ~100% as brains learn that betrayal is always punished. Click a person and check their inspector: the red curve (partner betrayed last) stays flat at zero — the grudge is real.
  • No honor: same world, retaliation reflex off. The society rots into suspicion (~15% cooperation, invites dry up). This is what pure selfish intelligence does without the norm.
  • Classic PD lab: textbook payoffs, no reflex, one-shot meetings — the bleak original. Compare with Reality: same minds, different institutions.
  • Turn locality to 0 (global mixing) or gossip circle to 0 (intel for everyone, even betrayers) — which social fabrics protect the cooperative equilibrium?
  • Raise temptation T to 9: how much quick money can the norm survive?
  • Amnesia: no memory, no gossip. Can the reflex alone sustain trust without record-keeping? (Watch what breaks.)

Honest limits: the retaliation reflex is the one hand-coded norm (everything else is learned); without it, independent learners can't bootstrap trust — that's a real finding, not a bug. Foresight (γ) only extends inside a meeting; cross-meeting consequences are felt through slowly shifting trust and acceptance. Everyone starts from random weights with diverse temperaments, so early history is noisy — judge "what intelligence does" only after the entropy chart falls.
